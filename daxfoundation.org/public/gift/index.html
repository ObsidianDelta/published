A note while we build

The DAX Foundation is newly established. Its purpose: the preservation, and then the expansion, of life, humanity, and consciousness.

Right now we are deliberately moving slowly in public. Before we ask anyone to trust us, we are putting the basics in place properly: verified identity, strict email authentication on this domain, and privacy and security built into how things work, not promised afterwards. If you've received something from us, it came from a domain that can prove it.

What we're researching

  • Sound: a shared language of feeling, built with people from many cultures.
  • Movement: gesture and motion as a way to learn and communicate, without giving up who you are.
  • Memory and culture: helping elders and communities keep their stories in their own words, attributable and theirs.

Everything is a research preview: unfinished on purpose, and shared so it can be held accountable from the start.

Who's behind it

Jason Jeyanandan is the Managing Director of Obsidian Delta and is serving as the Foundation's interim principal while it is set up, including the transfer of intellectual property from Obsidian Delta to the Foundation. His focus: how new technologies can be researched with accountability and responsibility.
