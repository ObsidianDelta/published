*The next iteration of [Human Delta Value](/blog/human-delta-value/). The first version stays up, unchanged and still reachable; [how I publish](/blog/how-i-publish/) says why.*

I always said I would come back to Human Delta Value, and this is me coming back to it. Not a correction. A refinement. I had material in my own notes that I had forgotten I had written, and when it surfaced I brought it back in, re-synthesised the whole of it, and sat with it for a while. The [previous version](/blog/human-delta-value/) is not wrong. This one is further along. The old one stays exactly where it is, for the reason [how I publish](/blog/how-i-publish/) gives: the record of the change is part of the point.

Here is where the thinking has got to.

---

Every system that works on people eventually has to answer one question: what did the person bring?

Not the tool. Not the model. Not the process. The person. If you cannot point at the part of the outcome that exists because *that* human was in the room, you have lost track of them. And a system that cannot say what the human brought has stopped extending people and started replacing them.

I needed a word for that part. I call it Human Delta Value.

The delta is the oldest symbol for change there is. Mathematicians have used it for centuries to mean exactly one thing: the difference between before and after. I am using it for exactly that, pointed at one person. Human Delta Value is every which way a human being can grow, added up. It is the change that happens *because you were involved* — the piece no one else could have made, because no one else is you.

That is also what DAX is. Not an acronym. Not three letters standing in for three words. DAX is how you say the delta out loud. The name is the symbol, and the symbol is the change a person brings. Preservation and expansion of life, humanity, and consciousness — that is the delta at the scale of all of us.

Then there is the dot.

A capable enough intelligence takes the waiting out of things. It can model one person, fit a way of seeing to them, deliver it through a channel they already trust, and check whether it took — in seconds, for everyone at once. That moment is coming whether we are ready or not. The dot is the point. It marks the instant when it matters most that humanity was not caught unprepared — and it is the commitment that we won't be.

Put them together and Δ· says the whole thing in two marks. The delta is you. The dot is the moment. The Foundation exists in the space between them: to make sure that when the moment arrives, every human being arrives at it larger than they were, and able to say what they brought.

---

One more thing, and I am marking it clearly as thinking in progress rather than a decision I have made: I have been turning over a fuller mark, O Δ · — an Obsidian ring, then the delta, then the dot. Read left to right it says Obsidian Delta to DAX Foundation. For it to hold, the ring has to stay a ring — the ground the rest is built on, the substrate — and never a letter. I am not settled on it, and I am not adopting it here. I am thinking out loud, which is what this site is for.

Human first. The rest follows from that.
