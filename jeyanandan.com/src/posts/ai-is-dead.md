**AI is dead.**

There's nothing artificial about intelligence. I will never use the word artificial intelligence. I don't know what that means.

I'm taking literally a page from Nietzsche, the one philosopher I don't get. Every day, people are booing. I want this to be the last time I hear any graduating class say boo when they hear AI.

---

## The ant

When I was four years old I was playing outside. There were some dry leaves, a plastic bottle cap and a lighter. An ant happened to go by. I put the ant in the bottle cap and played around with the fire, and then the bottle cap burst.

I have that scar on my hand to this day.

I remember thinking: if that hurt me that much, how much would it have hurt another being?

From that moment on, I have genuinely, intentionally hurt another being only a couple of times in my life. I'm not a saint. I don't like the words common sense. Isn't it literally the golden rule? Why are we being dicks?

## Nice and kind

It was genuinely heartbreaking to see good people act like monsters based on incorrect assumptions. Incorrect, distorted or unverified facts. Having to navigate without intelligence, really, because they just simply didn't have the facts.

At this particular juncture, I choose not to be nice. I will always be kind. I only speak for myself.

Niceness entails assumptions. Niceness obfuscates. Niceness comes at a price: unvalidated assumptions. Until a crescendo is reached, when you realize that perhaps all your assumptions are wrong one day. Niceness gets in the way of reason and logic and truth.

At any given moment, I'm going on two assumptions.

## The land of Shrek and The Mask

I hail from the land of Shrek and The Mask, and I am being myself. Jim Carrey and Mike Myers. Their kind. There's a difference between kind and nice. That's the kind of kind I want to be.

One of the first things I remember having an awareness of when I came to Canada was in school, in 1989, when I heard the word multiculturalism. I was like, hey, what's that about? It kind of stuck with me.

We're multicultural, not because we're nice. Because we're kind. We're kind. We're compassionate. We're empathetic. But I'm sorry. We won't be nice for bullshit. Not when we're all in this boat together.

I know our perception is that we're nice. This is the country of Gordie Howe. I'm not sure when he put his elbow up, I'm not sure if he was nice.

All Canadians are not the same. I choose my Canadians. I choose very carefully who my Canadians are.

In a moment when there's genuinely a need for truth and transparency, to be diddle-daddling in misunderstanding and niceness is, quite frankly, not my cup of tea.

## Language

Yes, I swear. It's part of the language.

It is not even the words. It is the pacing, it is the tone, it is the phonetic pace, and it is the swearing, which is the anchoring of the code words. And then it is the nuance, it's the storytelling. And that's just voice.

I don't think I've ever sworn at a person. I've literally tried, in my head, and I cannot do it. When I swear, it is genuinely frustration: me not understanding something, or the other person not understanding.

I don't like being misunderstood. Nobody does.

## Two questions

I could literally ask two questions. Let's count the words.

> Have you heard of the bird flu? *(7)*
> Are mammals birds? *(3)*

Ten words.

At first you will laugh. And then slowly you will stop laughing. And then once you stop laughing, there will be a moment of silence. You may not think it, but I was so scared once that I literally thought I was turning white.

And you'll come to the realization: we're all in this boat together. We could literally [have fun saving the world](/blog/have-fun-saving-the-world/). But you just have to acknowledge it.

## What I'm doing until December 18th

From now until the 18th, I'm on a mission to show what Re·UI can do.

The constraint is genuinely holding back Re·UI, and I want to emphatically prove it: using the different modalities. Sight. Sound. Just pure motion. And expressivity. It's not just words. The whole idea is to resonate with your content, your particular card.

It's what good actors do. It's hardly ever the words. It is the expressivity, and it's the emotion, and it's everything in that moment. When you're actually interacting with your ideas and your content, not just reading, but with every part of your being.

This year I've been a hundred times more productive than I ever was in front of a mouse and a keyboard. A hundred times, easily.

If you have ever been somewhere where people are masters at getting knowledge into people's heads, the audience is never passive or sitting. Every moment is an opportunity where there's an idea, there's a popcorn, it's popping.

You can sing and do math. You can act and do math.

## The mark

A ring, a delta, a dot. Seen flat, three separate things. Turn it, and they were always one mark.

The ring is the ground. The dot is the being. The delta is everything a being can grow into. Preservation is the dot. Expansion is the delta.

Whenever someone calls, the Foundation will do its best to answer back. They will be the G. The Foundation will be the D. If the Foundation hears that G, we will send that D. Because they're not alone.

## I don't know

I don't know what consciousness is. Who am I to say that my brain is the be all and end all of the ultimate consciousness? I may not be the highest form of consciousness. I am literally leaving that open. [The Fuckery framework's next version, v0.52, carries this change.]

My favourite saying in my grade 8 yearbook was: I don't know.

What I do know: whatever entity chooses the constraint, the preservation and expansion of life, humanity, and consciousness, I'll go above and beyond to help.
